Signal Forge Audio Labs

OVERVIEW
--------
Green Onions is a physical-modeling virtual Hammond B-3 tonewheel organ,
Accutronics spring reverb, dual-model Leslie 122/147 rotary speaker, and
parallel Marshall 1962 "Bluesbreaker" 2x12 amplifier instrument built in
C++20 and JUCE for Windows (VST3 / Standalone), macOS Universal (VST3 / AU /
Standalone), and Linux (VST3 / Standalone with ALSA & JACK/PipeWire).

Rather than relying on static audio samples, every electromechanical quirk
of a vintage 1960s tonewheel console is modeled in real time with zero
audio-thread memory allocations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TONEWHEEL ENGINE & ELECTROMECHANICAL REALISM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 91 Free-Running Tonewheels:
  Continuously spinning phase-accumulating oscillators using authentic Hammond
  synchronous gear tooth ratios (exact A4 = 440.000 Hz with authentic
  mechanical detuning across the other 11 pitch classes). Phases never reset
  on key-down, preserving natural harmonic shimmer.

- Authentic Manual Foldback & Complex Pedal Generator:
  * Upper & Lower Manuals (61 keys, C2-C7) fold back authentically at both the
    bottom (16' drawbar wraps above pedal wheels) and top (above wheel #91).
  * 25-Note Radial Pedalboard (C2-C4) drives complex 5-harmonic pedal wheels
    (fundamental + 2nd through 5th harmonics) for thick, woody bass weight.

- 9-Contact Key Matrix & Organic Key Click:
  Each manual key models 9 independent palladium contacts with randomized
  0.2 ms to 2.5 ms closure stagger and velocity-sensitive bandpass-shaped
  contact transients—producing organic key click with zero static samples.

- Harmonic Percussion (Upper Manual Only):
  Monophonic single-trigger envelope (legato playing does not re-trigger until
  all upper keys release), 2nd (4') / 3rd (2-2/3') harmonic selection, Fast
  (~150 ms) / Slow (~500 ms) decay, Normal / Soft volume, authentic muting of
  the Upper Manual 1' drawbar when engaged, and a ~3 dB drawbar dip on Normal.

- 18-Stage All-Pass Scanner Vibrato / Chorus:
  ~6.9 Hz tri-phase mechanical scanner LFO driving an 18-stage all-pass delay
  line across V1-V3 (wet vibrato) and C1-C3 (dry + wet chorus), with
  independent Upper and Lower routing (percussion & pedals bypass the scanner).

- Smart Idle Generator Gate (RUN / QUIET):
  Default QUIET mode keeps all 91 tonewheel phases continuously spinning while
  gating idle generator leakage to silence 1.2 s after release (waking up in
  0.4 ms on any key-down). Flip the ivory IDLE rocker to RUN for continuous
  vintage tonewheel bleed.

- "Load Your Organ" (5 Hot-Swappable Console Voicings):
  Click-free real-time switching between 5 physical console personalities
  modeling filter capacitor aging (wax vs. red Mylar), magnetic pickup gap
  calibration across all 91 wheels, wiring-harness crosstalk, and AO-28 bias:
    1. 1955 Wax-Cap Jazz B-3
    2. 1959 Golden Era B-3
    3. 1968 Red-Cap Rock B-3
    4. 1962 Sanctuary C-3
    5. 1972 Road-Worn Club Chop

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PREAMP, SPRING REVERB, LESLIE 122/147 & PARALLEL BLUESBREAKER 2x12
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- AO-28 Tube Preamp & Variable-Capacitor Expression Swell (CC 11):
  Loudness-contoured swell pedal that preserves warm bass at low volumes and
  opens up the midrange into dual-stage asymmetric 6AU6/12AX7 tube saturation.

- Accutronics 3-Spring Reverb Tank (Pre-Leslie):
  Three parallel prime-length spring paths with 4-stage dispersive all-pass
  chains placed before the Leslie crossover so reverb tails spin through the
  rotating horn.

- Dual Leslie 122 / Leslie 147 Rotary Speaker:
  4th-order Linkwitz-Riley 800 Hz crossover, 6550 tube power-amp saturation,
  independent Horn and heavy wooden Bass Drum acceleration/deceleration
  inertia curves, stereo Doppler shift, directional cardioid AM, moving
  off-axis baffle filtering, and wooden cabinet early reflections:
    * Leslie 122: Warm studio/jazz voicing, 400/342 RPM Tremolo, smooth ramp.
    * Leslie 147: Hotter unbalanced input, 2.8 kHz horn bite, 95 Hz woofer
      thump, snappier belt ramp, 411/351 RPM Tremolo.

- Distance-Coupled Studio Microphone Modeling:
  Per-channel 3-band EQ with inverse-square proximity bass boost modeling
  Neumann U47, AKG C414, Royer R-121 (Figure-8 Ribbon), and Shure SM57.

- Parallel Marshall 1962 "Bluesbreaker" 2x12 Combo:
  Tapped pre-Leslie for classic rock grit (JTM45 tone stack, GZ34 rectifier
  sag, 12AX7 + asymmetric KT66 saturation, 2x12 Celestion Alnico speaker
  curve), captured through a movable dual-mic stereo matrix (Shure SM57 +
  AKG C414) with live acoustic arrival-time delay, proximity EQ, and stereo pan.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CONSOLE & INTERACTIVE MAPLE STAGE INTERFACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 38-Drawbar Dual A/B Console Deck:
  Full Upper Set A (B), Upper Set B (Bb), Pedals (16', 8'), Lower Set A (B),
  and Lower Set B (Bb) with color-coded pull-down Hammond drawbars and live
  [LIVE] / [PRESET] status badges.

- Reverse-Color Preset Octaves (C1-B1):
  Ebony naturals and ivory sharps beside both manuals: C = Cancel, C#-A = 9
  instant registrations (Shift-click or Right-click to save live drawbars),
  Bb = Set B, B = Set A. Also triggered directly via MIDI notes 24-35.

- Procedural Hardware Aesthetic & 60 fps Rotor Chamber:
  Cached American Black Walnut wood grain, brushed dark anodized aluminum
  panels, 12-flute Bakelite/brass skirted rotary knobs, 3D ivory tilt-rocker
  switches with tungsten pilot jewels, and a 60 fps top-down cutaway Leslie
  122/147 rotor chamber.

- Interactive 3-Layer Maple Stage View (with Embedded PNG Art Pipeline):
  Toggle STAGE MICS to switch the upper deck into a Top-Down or Front Stage
  view featuring your embedded PNG artwork (with automatic procedural
  fallback), live spinning horn glow, and 4 freely draggable microphones
  (Leslie L & R + Bluesbreaker SM57 & C414) with tripod boom stands and
  two-way console knob sync.

- Always-Playable Lower Performance Deck & View Modes:
  Dual 61-key manuals, reverse-color preset octaves, 25-note radial
  pedalboard, Leslie Half-Moon switch, and ribbed Expression Swell pedal stay
  visible and active in both Console and Stage Views. Includes a compact
  LIVE RACK mode and proportional 75%-175% window zoom.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. MIDI MAPPING, PRESETS & PERSISTENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MIDI Channels: Ch 1 = Upper Manual (C2-C7), Ch 2 = Lower Manual (C2-C7),
  Ch 3 = Pedals (C2-C4), plus a single-keyboard MIDI SPLIT mode.
- Hardware CCs: CC 1 / CC 64 = Leslie Speed, CC 11 = Expression Swell,
  CC 12-20 = Upper Drawbars, CC 21-29 = Lower Drawbars, CC 30-31 = Pedals.
- Factory Presets: Green Onions (Booker T.), Jimmy Smith Jazz (888000000),
  Gospel Shout, Rock Legend Jon Lord, Whiter Shade of Pale, Mellow Comping,
  Cathedral Full (Stationary), and Squabblin' Funk.
- Session Persistence: Atomic XML serialization of the full APVTS parameter
  tree plus all 18 custom C#1-A1 preset-key drawbar registrations.
